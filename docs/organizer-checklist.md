# Organizer and team-lead checklist

Request schema descriptions or synthetic/irreversibly anonymized examples only. The owner sends this checklist; the builder sends no messages to the team lead.

- Exact B.Tech/PGDM column names, types, identifier format and length, enrollment uniqueness scope and source keys.
- Semester/trimester/year structure; credits, subjects, CGPA, grading and pass rule; PGDM duration and specializations.
- Placement company/role/package units, currency, year, offer statuses, multiple-offer policy and eligible-student denominator.
- Name scripts/transliteration, date formats, missing markers, institution/program codes and correction policy.
- Row/file volumes, PII classification, a synthetic sample and schema-only documentation.
- ERP export encoding, worksheets, full versus incremental export and update semantics.

After answers arrive, update assumptions, header mapping, program data, validators, grading rules and view definitions together, then regenerate synthetic fixtures and rerun all relevant tests. This does not authorize importing real personal data.
